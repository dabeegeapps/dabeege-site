Privacy Policy

AISLEPRO — Last updated August 31, 2026

AISLEPRO ("we", "our", or "the app") is a grocery list app that lets you tag items with store locations so shopping can happen without backtracking. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

The app works without creating an account. On first launch, your device is given an anonymous account so your list can sync and persist — this requires no personal information.

If you choose to sign in (optional), we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send magic link sign-in emails.
  • Name and profile picture — only if you sign in with Google or Apple and those services share them. We do not require or display these.

When you use the app, we store:

  • List and item data — the items you add, including name, brand, variety, quantity, unit, size, packaging size, notes, your own tags, and location tags (aisle, department, shelf).
  • Purchase history, including prices you enter — when you mark an item purchased you may record what you paid. We store that amount and the time of purchase, and read it back later to show what you last paid, to estimate a unit price, and to total the cost of a shopping trip.
  • Store data — the names of the stores you create and the order you arrange them in.
  • App preferences — settings like your selected store, language, and display options, stored on your device.

If you send a bug report or suggestion from within the app, we receive:

  • Your message and the type of report you chose.
  • An anonymous device identifier, so repeat reports from one device can be recognised. It is not linked to you and identifies nobody by name.
  • Technical details about the running app — app version, release channel, update identifier and its publish time, whether the build is running an update, platform and OS version, screen size rounded to the nearest 50 points, pixel ratio, text-scaling setting, and device locale.

What we deliberately do not collect:

  • Your device's name, which is frequently a person's real name.
  • Exact screen dimensions — they are rounded, because a precise value is close to unique to one device.
  • Location or GPS data. Aisle, department and shelf are labels you type; they are not derived from where you are.
  • Advertising identifiers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality (storing and syncing your lists, stores, and purchase history).
  • To suggest items to re-add based on your own purchase history.
  • To show what you last paid for an item, estimate unit prices, and total the cost of a trip.
  • To diagnose crashes and errors, and to understand and act on bug reports you choose to send.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account, list, purchase and feedback data are stored on Supabase servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign In — optional sign-in method. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting, used to find and fix faults. See sentry.io/privacy.
  • Resend — delivers our sign-in emails and notifies us when you send a report. See resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Your Choices

  • You can use the app without signing in at all.
  • You can delete individual items, stores and prices at any time from within the app.
  • Sending a bug report is always your choice; the app never sends one on its own.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.
