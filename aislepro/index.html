AislePro

Shop your list, not the whole store.

Shop without backtracking

AislePro is a grocery list app where every item is tagged with where it actually is in the store — aisle, department, shelf — so your list is sorted the way you'll walk the store, not the order you thought of things.

Add items with quantity and unit, organize them across multiple stores (each with its own layout), and check things off as Purchased or Skipped — skipped items move to your Library instead of just disappearing. Frequently-bought and suggested items make re-adding your regulars fast.
