Delete Your PackMaster Account

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open PackMaster
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This permanently deletes your account and all associated data, including your trips, items, custom fields and tags. This action cannot be undone.

Never signed in?
This still applies to you. PackMaster gives every device an anonymous account the first time you open it, and your lists are saved on our servers under that account even if you have never signed in. Deleting the account from Settings removes them, exactly as above.

Can't access the app?
Email us at dabeege.apps@gmail.com and we'll delete your account manually within 30 days.
