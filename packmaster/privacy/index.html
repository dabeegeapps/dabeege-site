PackMaster

Privacy Policy

PackMaster — Last updated September 14, 2026

PackMaster ("we", "our", or "the app") is a reusable packing-list app: you keep a library of items grouped by trip, and check them off as you pack. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

The app creates an anonymous account for your device the first time you open it, so your lists can be saved and kept private to you. That account holds no personal details.

If you choose to sign in, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send magic link sign-in emails.
  • Name — only if you sign in with Apple or Google and those services share it. We do not require or display it.

When you use the app, we store:

  • Your lists — the trips you create and the items in them, including names, quantities, units, sizes, notes, tags, and anything you type into a custom field.
  • App preferences — theme and other settings stored on your device.

We also collect:

  • Crash reports — if the app crashes, diagnostic data is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. This does not include the contents of your lists. See "Crash Reporting and Approximate Location" below for the one piece of location data this involves.

What We Do Not Collect

Stated plainly, because these are easy to assume:

  • No purchase records on our servers. Upgrades are handled entirely by the App Store and Google Play. We do not store what you bought or when.
  • No device identifier. The app does not create or send a persistent device or advertising ID.
  • No analytics, and no advertising. There is no analytics SDK in the app and no ads of any kind.
  • No location services. The app never asks for access to your device's location. The one exception is described below, and it comes from the network rather than from your device.
  • No notifications, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality — storing your lists and syncing them to your account.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account and list data are stored on Supabase servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign In — optional sign-in method. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. Sentry derives an approximate location from crash reports — see the section below. See sentry.io/privacy.

Crash Reporting and Approximate Location

When the app sends a crash report, your IP address is not stored on it. Sentry's servers do, however, derive an approximate location from the network the report arrives over — city, region and country — and store that on the crash event.

This is coarse, and it is not GPS. It comes from the network connection, not from your device's location services, which this app never requests. We use it only to tell whether a fault is specific to one region or one build.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app — see Delete Account for the steps. This removes your trips and items from our servers permanently, and it applies whether or not you ever signed in.

Children

PackMaster is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
